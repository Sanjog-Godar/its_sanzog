1. Introduction

Rudra Vasu Tech ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Maps for Clash - CoC Bases mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use the App.

By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

Important Disclaimer: This is an unofficial fan-made app. Clash of Clans is a trademark of Supercell Oy. This app is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Supercell.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Personal Information

  • Email Address: When you create an account or sign in
  • Display Name/Username: Your chosen username for chat and community features
  • Profile Information: Any information you provide in your profile

2.2 Automatically Collected Information

  • Device Information: Device type, operating system version, unique device identifiers
  • Usage Data: App features accessed, time spent in app, interaction patterns
  • Log Data: IP address, timestamps, crash reports

2.3 Data from Third-Party Services

  • Firebase Authentication: Email, user ID, authentication tokens
  • Firebase Cloud Firestore: Bookmarks, user preferences
  • Google Mobile Ads (AdMob): Advertising ID, device information, ad interaction data
  • Supabase: Chat messages and real-time messaging data

3. How We Use Your Information

3.1 Primary Uses

  • Account Management: Create and manage user accounts
  • App Functionality: Provide core features (bookmarks, chat, news, videos)
  • Personalization: Save preferences and customize your experience
  • Communication: Send notifications about app updates and features

3.2 Secondary Uses

  • Analytics: Understand app usage and improve features
  • Advertising: Display ads through Google AdMob
  • Security: Detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and security issues
  • Legal Compliance: Comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms

4. Data Storage and Security

4.1 Storage Locations

  • Firebase/Google Cloud: User authentication and database
  • Supabase: Chat messages and real-time data
  • Local Device: Cached data, bookmarks, preferences

4.2 Security Measures

  • Data encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS)
  • Secure authentication via Firebase
  • Access controls and authentication
Note: While we implement security measures, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Advertising and AdMob

6.1 How Ads Work

The App displays advertisements through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use:

  • Advertising ID: A unique, user-resettable ID for advertising
  • Device Information: Device type, OS version, screen size
  • Ad Interactions: Which ads you view or click
  • General Location: Country/region (not precise location)

6.2 Your Ad Choices

Opt out of personalized ads:

  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization
  • Reset Advertising ID: Settings → Google → Ads → Reset advertising ID

6.3 Ad Types in the App

  • Banner Ads
  • Interstitial Ads
  • Rewarded Ads
  • Native Ads
Important: We do not sell your personal information to advertisers.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at clashofbases.dev@gmail.com and we will delete such information.

8. Child Safety Standards

8.1 Reporting Child Safety Concerns

If you encounter any child safety concern in the App, including inappropriate content or behavior in chat:

  • In-App Reporting: Use the "Report" button available in chat conversations
  • Email: Contact us at clashofbases.dev@gmail.com
  • Response Time: We aim to respond to child safety reports within 24 hours
  • Authorities: We report child sexual abuse material (CSAM) to the relevant authorities as required by law

8.2 Community Chat Moderation

  • User reporting capabilities
  • Prohibition of sharing personal information (addresses, phone numbers, schools)
  • Prohibition of inappropriate, harmful, or abusive content
  • Account suspension for policy violations

8.3 Content Guidelines

Users must not:

  • Share personal contact information
  • Post sexually explicit or suggestive content
  • Engage in harassment, bullying, or threats
  • Impersonate others
Zero Tolerance: We have a zero-tolerance policy for child exploitation and abuse. Violations will result in account termination and reporting to law enforcement.

9. Your Data Rights and Account Deletion

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or export your personal data, to object to or restrict processing, and (in California) to know what is collected and to opt out of any sale. We do not sell personal data.

9.1 How to Delete Your Account

To request deletion of your account and associated data, follow the steps on our account deletion page:

https://sanjoggodar.com.np/account-deletion.html

In short: email clashofbases.dev@gmail.com from your registered email address with the subject "Account Deletion Request". We will complete the deletion within 30 days.

9.2 Other Requests

For access, correction, or export requests, email clashofbases.dev@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

10. Data Retention

  • Active Accounts: Data is retained while the account is active
  • Inactive Accounts: Data may be deleted after 2 years of inactivity
  • Bookmarks: Retained until deleted
  • Analytics: Aggregated data may be retained for up to 26 months

10.1 What Happens When Your Account Is Deleted

  1. Personal information (email, username, profile, bookmarks, preferences) is deleted within 30 days of a verified request
  2. Chat messages may be kept in anonymized form (username removed)
  3. Some data may be retained where required for legal or security purposes

11. International Data Transfers

Your information may be transferred to and stored on servers outside your country, including Firebase/Google data centers and Supabase data centers in various regions. We rely on our providers' safeguards for international transfers.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. Continued use of the App after changes means you accept the updated policy.
