About me

Professional summary

I'm a full‑stack web and mobile developer from Pokhara, Nepal. I work mainly with Flutter, Next.js, Laravel, Firebase/Supabase, and trading tools like Pine Script and MQL5. I enjoy taking an idea, turning it into a working app, and then improving it step by step.

I studied BSc.CSIT at Tribhuvan University and have worked with Websoft Technology Nepal Pvt. Ltd. Now I'm focused on client work and my own projects, including apps on the Google Play Store and new web apps I'm building for my portfolio.

I like clean, readable code and straightforward communication. If something isn't clear, I'd rather ask a few extra questions than guess. My goal is to build things that are actually useful, not just good on paper.
